A BRIEF INTRODUCTION

About Northeast India

Assam at the heart of a region full of routes, cultures and landscapes.

History & growth

Assam and the wider Northeast are connected by rail through important gateways such as Guwahati, Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Silchar and Naharlagun. These connections support daily travel, education, tourism, trade and family journeys across a geographically diverse region.

Role in India

Rail connectivity is especially meaningful in the Northeast, linking the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys with hill states and wider national rail corridors. The region’s landscapes, communities and economic centres make every route distinctive.

Digital reservations

A digital reservation workflow lets passengers search a route, choose a journey date, enter passenger information and receive a booking reference. A PNR (Passenger Name Record) is used to retrieve reservation details.
